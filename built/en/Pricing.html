What drives the quote? +

Module count, flow setup scope, data cleanup, existing-tool integrations, permission roles and any custom UI. Concrete items are listed after the demo.

Setup vs. monthly vs. usage — what is the difference? +

Setup is a one-time Phase-1 fee (configuration, data, integrations). Monthly covers the platform, upkeep and standard text AI. High-cost image/video AI is billed by usage.

Can we upgrade later? +

Yes. A → B → C stack. Upgrades keep your data and settings; only the new modules need setup.

Can we start with a single module? +

Yes. Start with the flow that hurts most, prove it, then expand.

Can we keep our LINE, CRM or forms? +

Yes. We take over the parts people used to carry between tools; the exact integration depends on tools, APIs and permissions.

When is custom development needed? +

Multiple system integrations, data migration, multi-team permissions, special approval flows or bespoke UI — custom items are scoped and quoted separately from the standard plan.

What does Phase 1 usually include? +

Goal and scope confirmation, module setup, data cleanup, internal testing and launch handover — detailed in the proposal.

What happens beyond the media allowance? +

Image and video are billed by actual usage. We estimate your range before rollout, so there are no surprise bills.

Terms and cancellation? +

Contract length, minimum term and adjustment terms depend on the plan — explained line by line before signing.
