Privacy Policy

Published: August 3, 2026

PeakQi International Ltd. (hereinafter "the Company" or "PeakQi") takes the protection of your personal data seriously. This policy explains how the Company collects, processes and uses personal data through www.peakqi.com (hereinafter "this website"), and the rights you can exercise.

1. What we collect, and how

When you contact us through this website's "Build your Phase-1 draft" or "rollout draft" forms, we collect the information you choose to provide, which may include:

  • Your name or preferred name, company name and industry
  • Contact details: phone, email or LINE ID (at least one)
  • The needs you describe, such as the flow you want to improve, the tools you currently use and any additional notes

Third-party services loaded by this website (for example, web fonts) may technically record general connection information; such information is handled by each provider under its own privacy policy.

2. Purposes of use

  • Responding to your inquiry, arranging scenario demos and rollout assessments
  • Preparing preliminary analysis and recommendations for the needs you raise
  • Follow-up business contact and customer service

Without your consent, we will not use your personal data for purposes other than those above, nor provide it to third parties for marketing.

3. Storage and management

Form data is stored in the Company's internally managed lead system, accessible only to business-related personnel within the necessary scope, and retained until the purpose of use is fulfilled or you request deletion.

4. Your rights

Under Article 3 of Taiwan's Personal Data Protection Act, you may exercise the following rights regarding your personal data with the Company:

  • Inquire about or request to review it
  • Request a copy
  • Request supplementation or correction
  • Request that collection, processing or use be stopped
  • Request deletion

To exercise any of these rights, please reach us through the contact channels below; we will handle your request within a reasonable period.
