Is this right for your team?

A fit

  • ✓Steady inquiry volume, data scattered across tools
  • ✓Support and sales keep re-organizing the same information
  • ✓Deals stall in chat logs with no next step
  • ✓Want to prove one segment first, then decide to expand
  • ✓Willing to name an internal owner for the rollout

Not yet a fit

  • —No clear process or real users yet
  • —Expecting AI to fully replace human judgment
  • —No usable data or content sources
  • —Prefer zero involvement — vendor decides everything
  • —Only need one-off content, no ongoing flow
