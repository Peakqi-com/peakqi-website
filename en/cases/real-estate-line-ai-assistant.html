Real-Estate AI Assistant (LINE OA)

A real-estate team whose listing, layout, price and viewing questions scattered across LINE, with replies taking hours. Integrated with the LINE official account, AI now answers listings and books viewings — negotiation and closing stay with the agents.

01 BACKGROUND — Client & industry

A business in real estate (presented anonymously; the client name and company size will be published once the client approves). This is a system PeakQi actually delivered and put live — screenshots are from the real product.

02 BEFORE — The workflow and the pain

Listing, layout, price and viewing questions scattered across LINE. Agents answered one by one and booked viewings by hand — replies often took hours.

03 SYSTEM — Modules deployed & integrations

AI answers listing questions and books viewings, integrated with the LINE official account.

MODULES
  • AI listing replies
  • Viewing scheduler
  • Lead records & follow-up reminders
INTEGRATIONS
  • LINE Official Account (live integration)

Additional integrations are scoped per project.

05 HUMAN — Review & exceptions

AI replies and books automatically; negotiation, property details and closing stay with the agent.

06 WORKFLOW — Inside the system
  1. Listing questions on LINE answered automatically
  2. AI books viewing slots
  3. Lead status recorded; follow-ups nudged
  4. Post-viewing reports and conversion tracking
